How it works

One score, built from four instruments.

The risk score isn't a guess — it's a weighted read of on-chain signals that historically separate sustainable tokens from rug pulls, refreshed every time a block confirms.

01 / PASTE

Drop in an address

Contract, token ticker, or wallet — NoRugs identifies the chain automatically.

02 / SCAN

Liquidity pools, holder distribution, contract functions, and deployer history, pulled live.

03 / WEIGH

Signals become a score

Each factor is weighted by how often it has preceded a real rug pull historically.

04 / EXPLAIN

Reading the gauge

Three zones. No ambiguity.

The needle never leaves you guessing — every score sits inside one of three clearly defined risk bands.

0 – 39
Low risk

Liquidity is locked, ownership is renounced or transparent, and holders are well distributed. Still do your own research.

40 – 69
Elevated risk

One or more yellow flags — concentrated holders, an unverified contract, or unaudited code. Proceed carefully.

70 – 100
High risk

Pattern strongly resembles historical rug pulls: unlocked liquidity, mint functions, or a deployer with a history of abandoned tokens.
